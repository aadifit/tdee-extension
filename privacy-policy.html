Privacy Policy — TDEE & Macro Calculator

Last updated: 2026-09-22

This browser extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, in any form, at any time.

  • All calculations run entirely inside your browser, in the extension popup.
  • The extension makes no network requests. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • No cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • No permissions are requested beyond showing the popup itself.
  • Values you enter (weight, height, age, etc.) exist only in that popup's memory while it is open, and are discarded the moment you close it.
